# TechCorp Company Profile

## Company Overview

**TechCorp** is a leading B2B SaaS company specializing in project management and team collaboration solutions. Founded in 2020, we have rapidly grown to serve over 5,000 customers worldwide, from startups to Fortune 500 enterprises.

### Mission Statement

## Product Suite

### TechCorp Workspace (Flagship Product)
An all-in-one project management and team collaboration platform that combines:
- Task and project tracking
- Real-time team communication
- File sharing and document collaboration
- Advanced reporting and analytics
- Workflow automation

### TechCorp Connect
Integration hub connecting 100+ third-party applications including Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Salesforce, and GitHub.

### TechCorp Mobile
Native iOS and Android applications for on-the-go productivity with offline capabilities.
